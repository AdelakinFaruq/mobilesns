MTN Airtime Transfer

  1. Dial *321# on the sender's MTN line
  2. Select Airtime Share / Gift
  3. Choose Share Airtime
  4. Enter the receiver's MTN number [09033966394]
  5. Enter the amount to transfer
  6. Input your MTN transfer PIN
  7. Confirm the transaction

Airtel Airtime Transfer

  1. Dial *321# on the sender's Airtel line
  2. Select Transfer Airtime
  3. Enter the receiver's Airtel number [09029192282]
  4. Enter the amount [20,000 MAX]
  5. Input your Airtel transfer PIN
  6. Confirm the transfer

Glo Airtime Transfer

  1. Dial *321# on the sender's Glo line
  2. Select Airtime Transfer
  3. Enter the receiver's Glo number [08154293313]
  4. Enter the amount
  5. Input your Glo transfer PIN
  6. Confirm the transfer


⚠️ Transfers only work within the same network. Ensure sufficient balance.
